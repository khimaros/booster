value menu corners

a menu like "remember drawer" or "home grid" at real size. the pill sits p(1) inside the menu, so its corners should curve parallel to the menu's, with a radius of the menu's minus p(1).

now

pill radius p(5)/2 (28dp) inside a p(3) (22dp) menu corner. the pill curves wider than the corner it sits in, so the gap pinches at the sides and opens at the corners.

a. concentric (recommended)

pill radius p(3) - p(1), which on this scale is exactly p(2) (13dp). the gap stays p(1) all the way round. one-line change.

b. full width band

no inset: the selection spans the menu and takes its corners when first or last. no longer a pill, which REQUIREMENTS.md names, so it needs your approval.

c. rounder menu

keeps the round pill and grows the menu corner to p(5)/2 + p(1) (37dp) around it. softer, and wider than the sheets' p(4) corners.
